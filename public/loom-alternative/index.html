When to use which

When Loom is the better choice

Pick the tool that fits the job. Loom is stronger when the video lives in a team workspace.

You need a share link

Loom gives every recording a hosted link with comments and viewer analytics. Simple Screen Recorder gives you an MP4 that you upload to Slack, Drive, Notion or email yourself.

Your team already lives in Loom

Shared libraries, permissions and integrations matter for large teams. Simple Screen Recorder is a personal tool without a workspace.

Choose Simple Screen Recorder when…

you want a polished demo with click zooms, a clean frame and an editable webcam, the recording must stay on your computer, or you do not want another account and subscription.

FAQ

Questions about switching from Loom

Short answers for people comparing the two.

Why is Simple Screen Recorder free?

Recording, editing and export run on your own computer, so there are no server costs to pass on. It is an independent product made by a product designer, and there is no paid plan today.

How do I save and share my videos?

When you stop recording, the editor opens in a new tab. Click Export to save an MP4 to your computer, then upload it wherever you need: Slack, Notion, Jira, Google Drive or email. There are no hosted share links.

Does it support audio recording?

Yes. You can record your microphone together with tab or system audio, and turn the microphone and camera on or off before you start.

Is there a watermark?

No. Exports have no watermark or logo.
